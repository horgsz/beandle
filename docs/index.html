How to play

Guess today's bean in 6 tries. Every answer is a real five-letter bean. 🫘

Type any five-letter word and press Enter. Each bean changes colour to show how close you are:

  • B Right letter, right spot.
  • E In the bean, wrong spot.
  • A Not in the bean at all.

A fresh bean sprouts every day at 12pm (midday) Sydney time. No spilling the beans to your friends! 🤫
