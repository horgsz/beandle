🫘 Beandle

How to play

Guess the bean in 6 tries. Every answer is a five-letter bean.

Each guess must be a valid five-letter word. After each guess the tiles change colour:

  • B is in the word and in the right spot.
  • E is in the word but in the wrong spot.
  • A is not in the word at all.

Whenever a new bean is planted you get a fresh game.
